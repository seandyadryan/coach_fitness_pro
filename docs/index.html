Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 October 2026 · Version 1.0

PT Deploy Dulu Pulang Nanti (“we”, “us”) develops Coach Fitness PRO, an Android fitness application with package ID com.deploydulupulangnanti.coachfitness. This policy explains how the app and our support channel handle information.

Information stored on your device

The app stores completed workout names, dates and durations; an unfinished workout session; daily water totals; your weekly session goal; water target; and experience preference. This information is stored in the app’s private local storage to provide the features you select. We do not receive it or send it to a server. The app does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, advertising identifier or account registration.

No advertising or tracking

This version has no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, tracking or third-party health SDK. It does not request Android network permission or read Health Connect, sensors, camera, microphone or location data. There are no in-app subscriptions. If you purchase the app, Google Play processes the purchase under Google’s own terms and privacy policy; the app does not receive your payment card details.

Retention, deletion and backups

Local records remain until you choose Profile → Delete all my data, clear the app’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall. Deleting data also removes any unfinished session. There is no developer server copy to delete or restore. Android automatic backup is disabled for the app. Your device and operating system security settings also affect protection of information stored locally. We cannot recover deleted local records.

Support email

If you choose to email seandyadryan2@gmail.com, your email provider sends your address, message and any attachments you include. We use these only to respond to and resolve your request, retain them only as necessary for that purpose or applicable legal requirements, and do not sell them or use them for advertising. Please do not send sensitive medical records. You may request deletion of support correspondence at the same email address, subject to legally required retention.

External services and this website

The app can open your email application and this public policy in your browser after your action. Those services operate under their own policies. This policy website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Browser requests may be processed by GitHub, including IP address and standard request data, under the GitHub privacy statement. This page sets no analytics cookies and contains no tracking scripts.

Adults and fitness information

The app is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children. It provides general fitness guidance, not diagnosis, treatment, medical advice or emergency assistance. Consult a qualified professional for individual health concerns, injuries or pregnancy. Stop exercising if you experience pain, dizziness or unusual shortness of breath.

Your choices and questions

You can use the app offline without creating an account. You control local entries and can delete them at any time. For questions, requests about support correspondence or applicable privacy rights, contact seandyadryan2@gmail.com. Please identify Coach Fitness PRO in your message.

Policy changes

We will update the effective date and this page if our practices change. Any future feature that sends information off your device will require a policy update and any applicable disclosures before use.
