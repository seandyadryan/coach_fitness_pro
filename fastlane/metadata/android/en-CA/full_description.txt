Show up. Get stronger. Make fitness part of your everyday life with Coach Fitness PRO.

Find a purposeful session, follow clear movement cues and keep a record of your effort. Designed for adults who want a simple training companion at home or in the gym.

TRAIN WITH A PLAN
Explore 8 structured routines covering full-body strength, core control, low-impact cardio, lower-body training, upper-body training, dumbbell basics and gentle mobility. Choose a short reset or a longer session at your own pace. Most routines need no equipment; Dumbbell Essentials uses dumbbells.

LEARN THE MOVEMENT
Browse 21 exercises with written form cues, equipment information and comfortable alternatives where appropriate. Read the guidance before starting and choose an intensity that suits you.

STAY IN THE SESSION
Use the exercise countdown, 30-second rest periods, pause and resume controls, and the option to move to the next exercise. Your unfinished session is saved locally and pauses when you leave the app.

SEE YOUR CONSISTENCY
Keep a local journal of completed workouts and actual session duration. See your weekly activity, session total, training minutes and current day streak. Set your own weekly session goal and experience preference.

BUILD EVERYDAY HABITS
Log water in 250 ml steps and choose a personal daily tracking target. Water entries reset with the local calendar day. Targets are for personal tracking, not medical recommendations.

OFFLINE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No subscriptions. Workout history, water totals and preferences stay on your device. Delete all local information in Profile whenever you choose. There is no cloud sync or automatic backup, so uninstalling removes your records.

Native Android design adapts to phones, tablets and resizable windows. The training content and default interface are in English. Store listing translations describe this same app.

Coach Fitness PRO provides general fitness guidance for adults aged 18 and over. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. If you have a health condition, injury or are pregnant, consult a qualified professional before exercise. Stop if you feel pain, dizziness or unusual shortness of breath. Results vary with individual circumstances.
